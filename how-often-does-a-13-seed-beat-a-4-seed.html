How often does a 13 seed beat a 4 seed?

About 21 times in 100. The 13 seed has beaten the 4 seed 33 times in 160 games since 1985. It happens most years, and it is still a clear step down from the 12 seed.

How is a 13-over-4 different from a 12-over-5?

It happens about half as often. The 12 seed has 57 wins since 1985. The 13 seed has 33. Both happen most years, but the 12 seed is the safer upset pick.
