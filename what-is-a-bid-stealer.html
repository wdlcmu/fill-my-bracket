Common questions

What is a bid stealer?

A bid stealer is a team that would not have earned an at-large bid but wins its conference tournament and claims the automatic bid instead. Each steal leaves one fewer at-large spot for bubble teams.

What is the most famous bid stealer?

NC State in 2024. The Wolfpack won the ACC tournament to secure a bid it would not have earned at large, then made a run to the Final Four.
