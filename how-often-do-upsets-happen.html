How often do upsets happen in March Madness?

Every year. Since 1985, the lower seed has won about one first-round game in three. Picking all favorites is not the safe play it looks like.

The 12-over-5 upset is the famous one.

It hits about one time in three.

The 8-vs-9 game is a coin flip.

In fact, 9 seeds win it a bit more than half the time.

The two wins nobody forgets

A 16 seed has beaten a 1 seed twice.

UMBC beat Virginia in 2018.

Fairleigh Dickinson beat Purdue in 2023.

More than 160 tries. Two wins.

That is the biggest upset in the sport.

What happens late

Upsets thin out as rounds go on.

Since 1985, 1 seeds have won more titles than all other seeds put together.

A 5 seed has reached the final four times and lost every time.

No 5 seed has ever won the title.

The lowest seed to win it all is 8.

That was Villanova, in 1985.

What this means for your picks

  • Take one or two 12 seeds and an 11 seed in round one.
  • Treat 8 vs 9 as a guess. History gives no real edge.
  • Keep your champion safe. Pick a 1 or 2 seed to win it all.
