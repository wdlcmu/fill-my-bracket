Common questions

What is bracketology?

Bracketology is the practice of predicting which teams make the NCAA tournament field and how they are seeded, and sometimes who wins each game. NCAA.com calls it a made-up ology. In 2027, the field being predicted holds 76 teams.

Who invented bracketology?

Reports credit ESPN analyst Joe Lunardi. The word bracketologist appeared beside his name in The Philadelphia Inquirer in February 1996, and his bracket projections reached ESPN the same year. NCAA.com's own predictor is Andy Katz, whose final 2019 prediction named 67 of the 68 teams in the field.
