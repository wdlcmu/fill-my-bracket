How a mock run works

1. You answer. Five short answers: upsets, tiebreakers, your team, your boosts.

2. It fills. All 75 picks — the 12 opening-round games, then all 63 games to one champion. Every pick gets a one-line reason that names the answer that drove it.

3. You fix it. Tap the other team in any game to flip that pick. Later rounds move only where they have to, and the page tells you what moved.

4. You keep it. The poster prints your full mock: champion, Final Four, every pick. Then run it again — test runs are the point.
