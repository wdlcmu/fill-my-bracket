What is a bracket pool?

A bracket pool is a friendly contest: everyone fills out a March Madness bracket before the games start, right picks earn points as the real games finish, and the bracket with the most points when one champion is left wins. Most pools are free, and most are played for bragging rights.

How a bracket pool works

1. Everyone fills a bracket. One winner picked in every game, all the way to one champion. Brackets lock when the first main-bracket games start: Thursday, March 18, 2027, for the 2027 tournament. The field is set a few days earlier, on Selection Sunday, March 14, 2027.

2. Right picks earn points. In the common system the points double each round: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 32 for picking the champion. The full walk-through is on the scoring page.

3. The best bracket wins. Because late rounds are worth the most, pools are usually decided by the Final Four and the champion, not by the first weekend. A pool can be three friends in a group chat or a whole office.

What is a bracket pool?

A bracket pool is a friendly contest built on March Madness brackets. Everyone in the pool fills out a bracket - a winner picked in every game - before the first games start. As the real games finish, each right pick earns points, and the bracket with the most points when one champion is left wins the pool. Most pools are free and played for bragging rights among friends, family, or coworkers.

How is a bracket pool scored?

The common system gives points for every right pick, doubling each round: 1 point in the first round, then 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 for the champion. Later rounds are worth more, so a bracket can survive early misses if its Final Four and champion are right. Some pools add upset bonuses. The scoring page on this site walks through the numbers.

Do I need to know basketball to join a bracket pool?

No. A bracket is preference picks, not a test. Plenty of winning brackets are built on seeds, mascots, colors, or one team the picker loves. On this site, five answers fill a whole bracket for you, with a reason on every pick, so a first-timer's bracket is as complete as anyone's.

Does Fill My Bracket run bracket pools?

No. Fill My Bracket fills out your bracket; it does not host pools, take entries, or handle prize money. You print or share your finished bracket and enter it in your pool wherever the pool lives - an office contest, a family group chat, or a pool site.
