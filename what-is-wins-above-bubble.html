Common questions

What is Wins Above Bubble?

Wins Above Bubble, or WAB, is a results-based metric that shows how many more or fewer wins a team earned than a bubble team would average against the same schedule. The NCAA committees use it beside the NET rankings.

What is the difference between WAB and NET?

The NET is a predictive metric based on efficiency and opponent strength. WAB is a results metric: it ignores margin and efficiency, and counts only what a team actually won against its schedule.
