Common questions

What is an automatic bid?

An automatic bid is the March Madness spot a team earns by winning its conference tournament. Every Division I conference tournament winner gets one, whatever its regular-season record was. The selection committee does not choose these teams.

Can a team with a losing record get an automatic bid?

Yes. NCAA.com says the winner earns the trip regardless of regular-season performance. Win the conference tournament, and the spot is yours, even after a losing regular season.
