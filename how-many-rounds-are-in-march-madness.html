Common questions

How many rounds are in March Madness?

In 2027 there are seven stages: the Opening Round, then the First Round, Second Round, Sweet 16, Elite Eight, Final Four, and the championship game. The main bracket that fans pick holds six of those rounds, starting with 64 teams.

How many games must a team win to be champion?

A team that starts in the main bracket must win six straight games. A team that starts in the Opening Round must win seven straight, because it has to win its way into the main bracket first.
