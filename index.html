Common questions

Do I need to know basketball?

No. You answer five simple questions about what you like. The tool uses your answers to pick each game. No basketball knowledge is needed.

When can I fill my bracket?

March Madness teams are announced on Selection Sunday. That is Sunday, March 14, 2027. Come back that evening. Fill your full bracket before the first games start.

Is it free?

Yes. Fill My Bracket is free forever. No ads. No accounts.

What is a seed?

A seed is a team's rank in its region. Seed 1 is the strongest team there. Seed 16 is the weakest there. First games pair high seeds against low seeds.
