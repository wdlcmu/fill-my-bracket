When is Selection Sunday 2027?

Selection Sunday is Sunday, March 14, 2027. The full field is announced on TV that evening. Brackets open right after the show.

What happens that night

A committee names each team in the field.

Each team learns its seed and its first game.

You have about three days before the first round.
