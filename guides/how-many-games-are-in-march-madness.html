How many games are in March Madness?

March Madness has 75 games in 2027. The main bracket holds 63 of them. The new Opening Round adds 12 more.

The count, round by round

Every game sends exactly one team home.

So 76 teams need 75 games total.

Why the total grew in 2027

Through 2026, each tournament had 67 games.

The First Four added four games then.

The field held 68 teams in those years.

In 2027, the field grows to 76.

The Opening Round now holds 12 games.

That adds eight games to the total.

The main bracket count never changed.

It has held 63 games since 1985.

Common questions

How many games are in March Madness?

There are 75 games in each 2027 tournament: 12 Opening Round games plus the 63 games of the main bracket. Through 2026, the total was 67 games, with 4 First Four games plus the same 63-game bracket.

How many games do you pick in a bracket?

You pick 63 games, the whole main bracket from the First Round to the title game. Most pools do not score the Opening Round games, which finish before the main bracket starts.
