How many teams are in March Madness?

From 2027, each March Madness tournament, men's and women's, holds 76 teams.

The short answer, and what changed

Through 2026, each tournament held 68 teams.

The NCAA announced a bigger field in 2026.

NCAA.com published the new format on May 7, 2026.

From 2027, each field grows to 76 teams.

It is the first men's expansion since 2011.

The women's field last grew in 2022.

How 76 teams become a 64-team bracket

The main bracket still holds 64 teams.

It still has the same 63 games.

24 teams play in the Opening Round.

They play 12 games over two days.

The 12 winners move into the main bracket.

They join 52 teams that waited.

Read how the Opening Round works.

How teams get in

Every conference champion earns an automatic spot.

The selection committee picks the other teams.

Those picks are called at-large bids.

The committee also gives every team a seed.

Seeds run from 1 to 16 per region.

The whole field is named on Selection Sunday.

In 2027, that is Sunday, March 14.

New to seeds? Read what a seed is.

How the field grew

The first men's tournament had just 8 teams.

The field reached 16 teams in 1951.

It reached 64 teams in 1985.

It grew to 68 teams in 2011.

Now it grows again, to 76 in 2027.

The women's tournament began in 1982, with 32 teams.

It reached 68 teams in 2022.

It also grows to 76 in 2027.
