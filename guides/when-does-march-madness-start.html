Common questions

When does March Madness start in 2027?

March Madness starts on Tuesday, March 16, 2027, with the Opening Round. The main bracket, the first round of 64 teams, starts on Thursday, March 18, 2027.

When is the field announced?

The field is announced on Selection Sunday, March 14, 2027. Each team learns its seed and its first game that evening, and brackets open right after the show.

When does March Madness end in 2027?

The tournament ends with the title game on Monday, April 5, 2027, in Detroit. The Final Four semifinals are two days earlier, on Saturday, April 3.
