Common questions

What is the NIT?

The NIT is the National Invitation Tournament, a single-elimination postseason tournament run by the NCAA for teams that miss the NCAA tournament field. In 2027 it holds 24 teams, and its champion is crowned at Detroit Mercy on Sunday, April 4.

Is the NIT the same as March Madness?

No. March Madness is the NCAA tournament, which crowns the national champion. The NIT is a separate tournament for the strongest teams left out, including the first four out of the NCAA field, who enter as No. 1 seeds.
