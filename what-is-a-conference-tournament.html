Common questions

What is a conference tournament?

A conference tournament is the postseason tournament each of the 32 Division I conferences holds after the regular season. The winner earns an automatic bid to the NCAA tournament, no matter how its regular season went.

Does the regular-season champion get the automatic bid?

No. The automatic bid goes to the conference tournament winner, not the regular-season champion. A team can finish near the bottom of its league, win the tournament, and still reach March Madness.
