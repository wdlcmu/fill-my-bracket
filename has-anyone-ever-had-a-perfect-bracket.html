The math, shown

Common questions

Has anyone ever had a perfect March Madness bracket?

No. No verifiable perfect bracket has ever been turned in. The longest proven streak is 49 straight correct picks, by Greg Nigl in 2019. His bracket broke in the Sweet 16.

What are the odds of a perfect bracket?

If every game were a coin flip, the odds are 1 in 9,223,372,036,854,775,808, about 1 in 9.2 quintillion. A bracket has 63 games, so there are 2 to the 63rd power possible brackets. NCAA.com puts the odds near 1 in 120.2 billion if you know basketball.
