Common questions

What is the Elite Eight?

The Elite Eight is the round of the NCAA tournament when eight teams remain, two in each region. Each game is a regional final, and the four winners advance to the Final Four.

When is the Elite Eight in 2027?

The men's Elite Eight is Saturday, March 27 and Sunday, March 28, 2027, in San Antonio, Los Angeles, Kansas City, and New York. The women's Elite Eight is Sunday, March 28 and Monday, March 29, in Philadelphia and Las Vegas.
