Bracket basics

How often does an 11 seed beat a 6 seed?

About 39 times in 100. The 11 seed has beaten the 6 seed 62 times in 160 games since 1985. Lately it is closer to a coin flip.

The recent pattern

Since 2013, 11 seeds have won 24 of 48 games against 6 seeds.

That is exactly half.

The early years were colder. From 1985 to 1999, 11 seeds won 18 of 60.

Nobody knows one clean reason. The wins are real either way.

Eleven seeds win again, too

27 of the 62 winning 11 seeds also won their next game.

That is about 44 in 100.

No other upset line keeps winning like this one.

Eleven seeds go deepest

Six 11 seeds have reached the Final Four.

LSU in 1986. George Mason in 2006. VCU in 2011.

Loyola-Chicago in 2018. UCLA in 2021. NC State in 2024.

No other double-digit seed has done it as often.
